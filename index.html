About Me

Bridging classroom teaching with campus-wide technology strategy

I am an Assistant Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at PIET , Parul University, and I currently serve as Manager of the university's ICT Cell. My work sits at the intersection of teaching, research, and institutional technology: in the classroom I teach programming, databases and data mining; across the university I help faculty adopt AI tools, learning-management platforms and digital teaching methods.

"Technology only helps a classroom when the people in it know how to use it well — that's the gap I try to close."

My research centres on machine learning and data mining, with recent work spanning emotion recognition, facial-feature authentication, image classification with CNNs, and text mining of customer feedback. I am currently pursuing my PhD at Parul University, and I hold an MCA from North Gujarat University, completed with distinction.

Outside the classroom, I've authored three books on C, C# and data mining, and I regularly train fellow faculty on AI tools for research, LaTeX, Mentimeter and the university's LMS — work that has taken me to AICTE ATAL Academy programs across the country.
